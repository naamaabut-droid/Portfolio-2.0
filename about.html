About Me

Designer by craft, builder by habit.

I'm a Senior Product Designer with roots in visual design and branding, and deep expertise in design systems. I care - sometimes obsessively - about the details and specs that make a product feel right, without ever losing sight of the bigger picture or the user behind it.

5 reasons to hire me:

  1. 1.Holistic, systems-level thinking. I design for the whole product as one system — not just the part in front of me — moving from the big picture down to the smallest detail.
  2. 2.Complexity into measurable impact. I untangle complex challenges and drive them to real, measurable results — every decision grounded in rationale and pressure-tested against alternatives, never just the first idea that comes up.
  3. 3.Full ownership you can trust. I take complete ownership of everything I work on — self-directed and dependable, the kind of hand-off you never have to chase.
  4. 4.Fast without cutting corners. I move quickly, pick up new domains and tools fast, and keep quality high — always leveling up and learning from the people around me.
  5. 5.A people person and ego-free team player. Strong communicator, open to feedback, and focused on the product winning — not on being right.

Bottom line: I bring real results and good energy to every team — the kind of teammate you actually want in the room.
